# Combined randomiser development

- Added independent Boss health and Miniboss health controls under In Dev, Enemy Health. Both default to following Enemy health for older presets. Explicit values override it. Minibosses include the game's MiddleBoss actors, Dead Hand and Iron Knuckles. Miniboss Lizalfos are separated from ordinary Lizalfos by their actor pack. Experimental, not tested in-game yet.

- Added experimental No Logic under In Dev. Items can be placed behind themselves and the seed may be impossible to finish. Your shuffle restrictions and fixed placements still apply. Spoilers list items without claiming a verified playthrough. Not available in Archipelago or tested in-game yet.

- Added an experimental Item Pool choice under In Dev. Balanced keeps the existing pool. Scarce replaces half the shuffled heart pieces and heart containers with Red Rupees, and Minimal replaces all of them. Starting hearts and unshuffled rewards stay as they were. Not tested in-game yet.
